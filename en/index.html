    Memory runs slower than it can

    The modules are rated for 6000 MT/s but run at 4800: the XMP/EXPO profile is off in the BIOS. Games limited by the processor feel this the most.

    1. BIOS → find XMP (Intel) or EXPO / DOCP (AMD).
    2. Choose Profile 1.
    3. Save and reboot — Forge checks the speed itself.
    The monitor runs at 60 Hz instead of 165

    The refresh rate can be raised right from Forge. If the screen goes dark, it comes back by itself in 15 seconds.

    Safety

    No risk to the game or the system

    Everything comes back

    Forge copies files before writing, and every action goes into the history. Undo always works, even without a subscription.

    Settings only

    Forge changes the same settings files as the game's own menu and never touches its memory or processes. It is not a cheat or a mod.

    Protection stays on

    Forge never turns off Windows Defender, updates or important services without your consent. Every tweak says what it gives and what it costs.
